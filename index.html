Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

This is a private upload tool used by the owner of a few YouTube channels to upload their own videos to their own channels through the YouTube API.

Data

The tool does not collect, store, share or sell personal data from other people. It only uses the owner's own Google login to upload videos and set titles, descriptions and thumbnails on the owner's own channels. Login tokens stay on the owner's own computer.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel owner through the channel's About page on YouTube.
